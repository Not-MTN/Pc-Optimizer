Keel installs for this user only — there is no administrator prompt, and it
cannot touch another account's settings.

It will add about 12 MB to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Keel. Your data (journal,
settings, undo history) goes in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Keel and survives upgrades.

Keel needs Windows 11 (build 22000 or newer). It does not install a browser,
a runtime, a driver or a service, and it makes no network connections.
