Running without administrator rights — settings that change Windows itself will be refused. Personal settings (colours, taskbar, wallpaper) still work.

Fifteen looks, one click

Each theme is a wallpaper, a widget layout, shell choices and a terminal palette in one file. Every tile is rendered from its own theme — what you see is what you get, not a stock screenshot.

Nothing here is forced. Every look below is a still image, so it costs 0% GPU once rendered — the widgets are baked in, not repainting. Anything animated would be opt-in and labelled with its cost.

Changes

Make your own

Paste a theme file someone shared. It is validated and diffed before anything is applied — a theme is somebody else's JSON.

Make it yours

Taskbar, Explorer, icons, colours and the prompt. Every switch names what it changes and what Windows 11 will refuse.

Make it faster

Measure first, change one thing, measure again. Nothing here runs on a timer.

When something breaks

Drivers, battery, hardware surprises and recovery. Read-only until you ask.

    Rules being enforced

    • Only measured costs are counted — unmeasured items are labelled unattributed.
    • A service with live dependents is refused, and the dependent is named.
    • Deferring is preferred over disabling: 60 seconds buys the win, not the breakage.
    • Whatever re-adds itself is reported next scan, with a guess at who.
