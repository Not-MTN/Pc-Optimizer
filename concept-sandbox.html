    What watch mode can and can't see

    Sees: file creates/deletes/moves (USN), registry writes (ETW), services, tasks, drivers, process trees, every network endpoint.
    Can't see: kernel-mode writers that hide from user mode — the optional T2 minifilter closes that gap. MSIX/UWP installs go through the deployment stack, so they get their own path.
    Never does: invent a clean bill of health. Missed areas are reported as blind spots.
